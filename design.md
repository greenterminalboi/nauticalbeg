## Brand & Style
The design system manifests as an authentic, high-prestige 16th–17th century imperial manuscript and portolan chart workshop. It eschews generic modern digital tropes in favor of material, historical substance: burnished ahar vellum, crushed-mineral lapis lazuli pigments, vermilion rubrication, hand-beaten gold leaf, and iron-gall inks. 

The aesthetic is Tactile & Skeuomorphic Historical Artifacture. It communicates imperial gravitas, meticulous archival authority, and the sublime craftsmanship of royal scriptoriums and naval hydrographers. Interfaces behave not as software viewports, but as preserved folios, charts, astronomical broadsheets, and bound imperial codices. Every boundary, clasp, seal, and illumination conveys physical mass, pigment granulation, and deliberate scribal intent.

## Colors
The palette derives purely from historical mineral pigments and organic parchment chemistry:

- **Primary (`#0a2540` / `#0d3b66`):** Natural Afghan Lapis Lazuli Ultramarine. Applied to imperial titles, celestial chart spheres, major navigational rhumb lines, and principal illuminated cartouches.
- **Secondary (`#b91c1c` / `#991b1b` / `#c2410c`):** Cinnabar & Vermilion Rubrication. Employed for rubricated drop caps, critical markers, warnings, ceremonial wax impressions, and section marginalia.
- **Tertiary (`#d97706` / `#f59e0b` / `#b45309`):** Burnished Gold Leaf & Brass. Used for gilding rules, serlevha floral arabesques, compass roses, corner hardware clasps, and raised metallic accents.
- **Accent Emerald (`#047857` / `#065f46`):** Ground Malachite Wash. Dedicated to terrestrial topographies, botanical borders, flourishes, and maritime coastal tinting.
- **Neutral (`#1c1917` / `#292524`):** Iron-Gall Brown-Black Ink. The foundational color for running body text, engraved cartographic grids, calligraphic rules, and technical data.
- **Surface Vellum (`#faf5ef` base, `#fef3c7` wash, `#fde68a` edge burnish):** Heavy Ahar and Calfskin Parchment. Backgrounds have the subtle tonal variation of hand-sized vellum with pale egg-white sizing sheen.

## Typography
Typographic discipline mirrors the scribal traditions of the Renaissance and early modern Levant/Europe:

- **EB Garamond** anchors all headers, illuminated captions, and authoritative seals. Small caps with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) are used for rubricated titles, sub-headers, and button glyphs, evoking hand-cut foundry metal sorts and manuscript titling.
- **Literata** provides high-legibility book text engineered for sustained reading on fibrous parchment backgrounds. It reproduces the natural warmth, sturdy serifs, and organic stroke modulation of quill and reed pens.
- **Rubricated Drop Capitals:** The initial letter of primary chapters or major folios spans 3 to 4 lines of body text, rendered in vermilion red (`#b91c1c`) or highlighted with a gold leaf floral surround.
- **Marginal Glosses:** Small body styles (`body-sm`) are rendered with italicized ink styling, placed in wide folio gutters to evoke handwritten commentary and archival apparatus.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system follows classical manuscript codex proportions and nautical chart architecture (the Van de Graaf canon combined with portolan radiating rhumb geometry):

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column symmetrical layout on desktop conforming to double-page spread proportions (recto and verso), collapsing to an unbroken single scroll or singular folio leaf on mobile devices.
- **Margins & Wide Gutters:** Margins are deliberately luxurious (`3rem` desktop) to evoke uncut deckled vellum pages. Outer page edges are wider than the inner binding margins.
- **Portolan Rhumb-Line Network:** Structural layouts and dashboards can feature faint, underlying 32-point navigational wind-rose lines (`rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.08)`) that guide column alignments and element intersections.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile` (< 768px): Margins step down to `1.25rem`. Complex dual-leaf folios split into sequential codex leaves with bottom-tethered brass tab navigation.
  - `Tablet` (768px - 1024px): 8-column layout with pinned margins preserving illuminated header cartouches.
  - `Desktop` (> 1024px): Full 12-column layout preserving golden ratio text-to-margin proportions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system is tactile and physical rather than optical or atmospheric:

- **Layered Vellum Sheafing:** Elements do not float on nebulous soft box-shadows. Instead, depth is achieved by stacking parchment leaves, characterized by subtle, crisp under-edge shadows (`1px 2px 3px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.22)`, followed by a secondary contact occlusion of `0 1px 1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.3)`).
- **Embossed & Burnished Gold Foil:** Gilded motifs, clasps, and border filigree use dual-directional bevels (top-left highlight `rgba(254, 243, 199, 0.8)`, bottom-right shadow `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.9)`) simulating raised gesso under gold leaf.
- **Wax Seals & Medallions:** Wax seals have prominent physical volume with sharp specular highlights on the seal lip, a deep recessed center matrix, and thick, uneven radial cast shadows (`0 4px 8px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.35)`).
- **Portolan Borders:** Elevation is demarcated horizontally by multi-ruled bounding frames: outer thick iron-gall line, alternating black/white parchment distance rulers, and an inner gold-and-vermilion ribbon border.

## Shapes
The structural geometry is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**, reflecting historically hand-trimmed parchment, clipped parchment folios, and brass book furniture:

- **Corner Morphology:** All main surfaces, cards, and dialogue plates possess square `0px` cut corners, frequently accentuated by engraved brass corner clasps (`corner-guards`) or ornate gilded filigree brackets.
- **Notched & Scalloped Cartouches:** Modals and callouts may adopt classical Renaissance escutcheon forms or Persian *serlevha* arch profiles at the top edge, while maintaining razor-sharp foundational corners.
- **Hardware & Fasteners:** Checkboxes, pins, and tabs mirror square-cut hand-hammered iron studs or chiseled brass hinges. Wax seals provide the only circular, hand-pinched exception within the structural architecture.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Clasps
- **Primary Imperial Clasp:** Styled as a cast brass and burnished gold lock (`#d97706` background with `#b45309` double-line border). Text is set in `label-md` all-caps iron-gall ink. Hover triggers a radiant gold-leaf burnish sheen; active/press causes a tactile 1px inward mechanical displacement.
- **Secondary Folio Action:** Rich Lapis Lazuli ground (`#0a2540`) with hand-ruled gilded borders and cream vellum lettering.
- **Wax Seal Stamp (Icon / Destructive Button):** Heavy cinnabar red circular medallion (`#b91c1c`) debossed with an imperial monogram or seal. Hover deepens the pigment; press mimics pressing a warm seal into parchment.

### 2. Illuminated Cards & Folios
- Constructed from a textured vellum surface (`#faf5ef`) framed with authentic portolan nautical scale-bar borders (alternating black-and-white nautical tick marks encased in dual hairline gold fillets).
- Headers feature hand-watercolored wash banners (lapis, malachite, or vermilion) topped with delicate arabesque pen-flourishes (*tezhip* vine motifs).

### 3. Text Inputs & Formulary Parchments
- Background is a paler calfskin hue (`#fffbeb`) framed by a single 1px ruled iron-gall border (`#292524`).
- Label is positioned as a rubricated vermilion sub-heading.
- Active focus replaces the border with an illuminated gold leaf fillet and introduces a subtle internal ink-well shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.15)`).
- Caret is a calligraphic broad-nib quill stroke rendered in deep ultramarine.

### 4. Checkboxes, Radios & Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** Square brass clasps with an engraved diagonal corner detail. Checked state displays a hand-drawn cinnabar red iron-gall cross mark or rubricated florish (`🞨`).
- **Radio Buttons:** Diamond-shaped brass studs (`45deg` rotated square) with a deep lapis lazuli dot embedded when selected.
- **Toggle Switches:** Reimagined as sliding brass codex clasps that slide across an inlaid ivory/ebony track.

### 5. Chips, Badges & Seals
- Small manuscript labels framed in decorative brackets (`[ Venice ]`, `{ Terra Incognita }`).
- Status indicators utilize miniature round wax wafers: Cinnabar Red (urgent/danger), Malachite Green (verified/active), and Lapis Lazuli (archived/sacred).

### 6. Lists & Tables (Imperial Registers)
- Rows alternate with faint malachite or gold-tinted watercolored washes.
- Separator rules resemble calligraphic pen-strokes that taper elegantly at both ends.
- First item of every ledger row leads with an ornamental calligraphic rubric mark (`§` or gilded initial).

### 7. Portolan Cartographic Overlays & Tooltips
- Modals appear as rolled nautical scroll charts with aged, deckled parchment edges and gilded weights anchoring the corners.
- Tooltips are miniature navigational cartouches held within miniature gold-bordered ribbon scrolls.
